      How ties get broken

      Two items with the same count: the one you picked when you compared those two goes higher. This is the tie rule from What Color Is Your Parachute?

      Three or more items with the same count: the book says to put them in order by gut. This page starts from your own picks among those items, and the one you picked most often among them goes higher. Any that are still tied go through the same steps again.

      Either way, "Compare these again" shows just the pairs among the tied items, with the picks you made. Leave them, or change any you see differently now. A change there only reorders the tied items, and the grid and the counts stay as they were.

      If you picked in a circle (A over B, B over C, C over A), none of them wins head-to-head, so change one of those picks to put them in order.

      Breaking a tie never moves an item above one you picked more often.

      To change a pick everywhere, counts included, go back to the grid and click its box.
